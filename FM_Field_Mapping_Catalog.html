Updates the account credit limit and credit class in CCBS after credit evaluation. Composed of three CCBS calls: GetAccountHeader (read current state), l9GetAccountCreditHistory (retrieve credit history), and ChangeAccountingManagementInfo (apply new credit limit). Corporate credit limits are calculated based on subscriber RC rates, grading multipliers, and IR installment amounts.

Credit Limit Calculation Logic
  • VIP Customer: flat 16,000 THB per subscriber
  • TOP/PREMIUM grading: ΣAgreement RC × 5 (or × 5/2 for IOT)
  • Standard: ΣSubscriber offer RC × multiplier from grading table
  • Add IR installment amount if present in order parameters
  • Floor: 1,000 THB · Rounding: nearest 100 THB
  • Waiver indicator set to Y if creditClass = 'W'
