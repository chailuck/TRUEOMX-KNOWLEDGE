Request_ATS_REGISTER_CAMPAIGN

ATS Campaign Registration — subscriber list aggregation with OrderType=12002 branching
Backend: ATS Author: DESKTOP-995HR2V Priority: 5 Pattern: FIRE-AND-FORGET Response: TRUELIFE_ID write-back
§1 — Overview & Purpose
ATS_REGISTER_CAMPAIGN registers the order's campaign in the ATS (Award and Targeting System) by sending a submitCampaignReq with requestName="RegisterCampaign". It aggregates ALL subscribers across all POU and ChildOU into a single subscriber list and sends them as productList entries.
Fire-and-forget: Uses Event.Ext.sendEventImmediate(reqEvent) + manual RequestCount++. Fan-in: count("000" responses) == RequestCount.
OrderType=12002 branching: OT=12002 uses subscriber-level name/identification fields instead of customer-level. OT=12002 sets convergenceType from ExtInfo, state="Installing", paymentType="Prepaid", familyType="MAIN". Non-12002 uses customer-level name, convergenceType="SpecialBundle", state="Active", paymentType="Postpaid".
Response write-back: OrderData.ExtendedInfo[Name="TRUELIFE_ID"] ← submitCampaignRes.truelifeId. Consumed by FM29 (CCBS_CREATE_GROUP) as groupName and FM30 (ATS_COMPLETED_CAMPAIGN).
