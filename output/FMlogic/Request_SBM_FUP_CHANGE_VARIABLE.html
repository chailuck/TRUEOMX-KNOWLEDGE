Request_SBM_FUP_CHANGE_VARIABLE

SBM FUP Add/Remove Variable SOC — iterates Agreement Offers per OU (POU + ChildOU)
Backend: SBM Priority: 5 Pattern: FIRE-AND-FORGET per OU Author: RS33-BANDIT Param: ADD | REMOVE
§1 — Overview & Purpose
SBM_FUP_CHANGE_VARIABLE adds or removes a variable SOC on SBM's Fair-Use Policy (FUP) service. One SBM_FUP_DO_SERVICE event is sent per OU (ParentOU or ChildOU) that has matching SPECIAL_OFFER_INDICATOR offers with FE_OR_CCBS=="FE".
Mandatory parameter guard: Parameter[0] must be "ADD" or "REMOVE" — any other value throws DATA_ISSUE immediately. function_id is dispatched: ADD→104300008; REMOVE→104300009.
BRMS filter + FE_OR_CCBS filter: Only Agreement Offers where FE_OR_CCBS=="FE" AND SPECIAL_OFFER_INDICATOR is in the global FUPAddVariableOfferInd list qualify. Matching OfferNames are concatenated into fupElement as the variableSOC parameter.
Hard fail on no FUP offer: If no matching offers are found in an OU that passed PreExecCheck, throws OMX_DATA_ERROR: "There is no FUP offer. OU: <OUId>".
Resubmit support: isActResub flag used; calls PurgePendingRequestsBeforeResubmit at the start of a resubmit run.
