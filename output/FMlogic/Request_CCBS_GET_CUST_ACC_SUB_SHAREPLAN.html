Request_CCBS_GET_CUST_ACC_SUB_SHAREPLAN

Fetches customer/account/subscriber info from CCBS for SharePlan — reuses CCBS_GET_CUST_ACC_SUB_ID event; iterates BOTH ChildOU and ParentOU subscribers

Priority: 5Target: CCBS Event: CCBS_GET_CUST_ACC_SUB_ID (reused) Scope: ChildOU + ParentOU (both) Author: DESKTOP-KB4BEG4
§1 — Overview & Purpose
CCBS_GET_CUST_ACC_SUB_SHAREPLAN retrieves customer, account, and subscriber data from CCBS for all SharePlan participants. Uniquely, it iterates both ChildOU subscribers first, then ParentOU subscribers. This is used in the APPLY_SHARE_PLAN flow to populate subscriber data for all SharePlan members. Reuses the CCBS_GET_CUST_ACC_SUB_ID event with SubNumber=MSISDN plus optional ParameterType from PRIMARY_RESOURCE_TYPE.
⚠ Dual-scope iteration order: ChildOU subscribers are iterated first (primary SharePlan children), then ParentOU subscribers (main/secondary). Most FMs iterate only ParentOU.
