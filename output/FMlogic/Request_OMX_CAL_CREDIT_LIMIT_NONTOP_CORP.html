OMX_CAL_CREDIT_LIMIT_NONTOP_CORP

Internal credit limit calculation — no backend call, computes PersonalCreditLimit from RC ExtInfo written by FM23
Backend: NONE (internal OMX rule) Author: warawich-nb Priority: 5 Pattern: Internal calculation → NextActivity Location: Rules.OMConsumers.OMXOM
§1 — Overview & Purpose
OMX_CAL_CREDIT_LIMIT_NONTOP_CORP is a pure internal calculation rule (OMXOM, not OMXFM). It derives a personal credit limit for non-TOP/PREMIUM corporate accounts from the RC (recurring charge) ExtInfo written by FM23 (OMX_GET_RECURRING_CHARGE). No external backend call is made.
No response handler: This rule completes synchronously and calls NextActivity directly. There is no corresponding Response rulefunction — the activity proceeds immediately after the calculation.
Credit limit calculation priority (per agreement type): 1. POU.Agreement.Offers ServiceType=80 + ServiceLevel=71/G → individual RC, RoundUp(RC, 100) 2. POU.Agreement.Offers ServiceType=80 + ServiceLevel=67/C → RC × numberOfSub, RoundUp to 100 3. POU.Subscriber.SubscriberOffers ServiceType=80 (FE only) → sum all RC + currentCreditLimit, RoundUp to 100 Same logic applies to ChildOU.Agreement.Offers and ChildOU.Subscriber.SubscriberOffers.
Write-backs (3): (1) account.AccountManagementInfo.PersonalCreditLimit ← calculateCreditLimit (if > 0). (2) AccountExtInfo Name="CALCULATECREDITLIMIT". (3) AccountExtInfo Name="CURRENTCREDITLIMIT".

§7 — Credit Limit Calculation Logic
PriorityConditionFormula
1 — Agreement individualPOU/ChildOU.Agreement.Offers: ServiceType=80, ServiceLevel=71 or G, FE_OR_CCBS=FERoundUp(RC, 100)
2 — Agreement corporatePOU/ChildOU.Agreement.Offers: ServiceType=80, ServiceLevel=67 or C, FE_OR_CCBS=FERoundUp(RC × numberOfSub, 100) (min numberOfSub=1)
3 — Subscriber offersPOU/ChildOU.Subscriber.SubscriberOffers: ServiceType=80, FE_OR_CCBS=FE; Agreement offers exhaustedRoundUp(sumRC + currentCreditLimit, 100)
currentCreditLimit: = account.AccountManagementInfo.PersonalCreditLimit IF it differs from OLD_PERSONAL_CREDITLIMIT (i.e., was already updated). Else 0.
OLD_PERSONAL_CREDITLIMIT: Read from Account.ExtendedInfo[Name="OLD_PERSONAL_CREDITLIMIT"].Value — written by an earlier CCBS GetAccountHeader step.
