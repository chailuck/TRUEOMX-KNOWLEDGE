§1 — Process Overview

Process: PREPAID_CANCEL — prepaid subscriber cancellation.
The flow gathers subscriber/account data (CRM, ASRM, INTX, CDB, CCP), computes refund offers, validates business rules, creates the CRM disconnect order, then conditionally provisions AA with a GeneralUpdate (CCD) for BRMS-flagged subscriptions and a Deactivate (DSD) for the main cancellation. Finally, releases MSISDN/SIM resources and confirms with AA.

§3 — Conditional Execution Rules (PreExecCheck)

3 CAT_GOD — execute only when offers exist

FM: CAT_GOD

count(//Offers) > 0 or count(//SubscriberOffers) > 0

Calls CAT (Catalogue) to resolve GOD-tier offer details. Skipped if subscriber has no active offers.

8 CCP_GET_PREPAID_CREDIT_INFO — skip for CCP channel

FM: CCP_GET_PREPAID_CREDIT_INFO

/ns0:OrderRequest/OrderData/Channel/text()!="CCP"

CCP channel already has credit info — skip the CCP lookup call.

9 OMX_CREATE_SUB_OFFER_PREPAID_REFUND — skip for CCP channel

FM: OMX_CREATE_SUB_OFFER_PREPAID_REFUND

/ns0:OrderRequest/OrderData/Channel/text()!="CCP"

Refund offer computation skipped when channel is CCP (no refund applicable).

11 CAT_GOD_B — execute only when CCP/BRMS_REMOVE offers exist

FM: CAT_GOD (2nd call)

(count(//Offers) > 0 or count(//SubscriberOffers) > 0) and //SubscriberOffers/ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and (Value='CCP' or Value='BRMS_REMOVE')]

Second CAT_GOD call for BRMS-flagged or CCP subscriptions that require an updated offer catalogue view after BRMS processing.

14 SBM_CANCEL_PREMIUM_NON_VOICE — only for Premium Number orders

FM: SBM_CANCEL_PREMIUM_NON_VOICE

boolean(//OrderData/ExtendedInfo[Name='PROJECT_NAME' and Value='PREMIUM_NUMBER'])

Cancels SBM premium non-voice services only when the order is for a Premium Number project.

17 OMX_GET_SRV_TRX_NO_CCD — BRMS_REMOVE subscriptions only

FM: OMX_GET_SRV_TRX_NO (not found)

boolean(//SubscriberOffers/ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and Value='BRMS_REMOVE'])

Gets a service transaction number for the CCD (GeneralUpdate) AA provisioning call. Only needed for BRMS_REMOVE offer type.

18 AA_PREPAID_PROVISIONING_CCD — GeneralUpdate for BRMS_REMOVE (param: CCD)

FM: AA_PREPAID_PROVISIONING

boolean(//SubscriberOffers/ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and Value='BRMS_REMOVE'])

Sends GeneralUpdate (CCD) to AA provisioning before the full deactivation. Only triggered for BRMS_REMOVE subscriptions.

19 AA_CHECK_CONFIRMATION_CCD — BRMS_REMOVE subscriptions only (param: CCD)

FM: AA_CHECK_CONFIRMATION (not found)

boolean(//SubscriberOffers/ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and Value='BRMS_REMOVE'])

Waits for AA to confirm the CCD provisioning. Same gate as steps 17 and 18.

21 AA_PREPAID_PROVISIONING — Deactivate (DSD) when IMSI present

FM: AA_PREPAID_PROVISIONING

count(//Subscriber/CdbProfile)>0 and count(//Subscriber/CdbProfile/IMSI)>0 and //Subscriber/CdbProfile/IMSI/text()!=""

Main AA deactivation. Requires an active CDB profile with a non-empty IMSI — confirms subscriber has a physical SIM registered in CDB.

22 ASRM_INVOKE_MSISDN_RELEASE — Normal cancel (not MNP reversal)

FM: ASRM_INVOKE_MSISDN (not found)

(//SubscriberActivityInfo/ActivityReason/text()!="MNPHR" and //SubscriberActivityInfo/ActivityReason/text()!="RFREOT")

Releases the MSISDN back to the pool for regular cancellations. Skipped for MNP handoff reversals.

23 ASRM_INVOKE_MSISDN_PORT_IN_REVERSE — MNP handoff reversal only

FM: ASRM_INVOKE_MSISDN (not found)

(//SubscriberActivityInfo/ActivityReason/text()="MNPHR" or //SubscriberActivityInfo/ActivityReason/text()="RFREOT")

Reverses the MNP port-in. Mutually exclusive with step 22 (exactly one fires depending on ActivityReason).

25 AA_CHECK_CONFIRMATION — not CCP/RM/ASRM channel + IMSI present

FM: AA_CHECK_CONFIRMATION (not found)

not(/ns0:OrderRequest/OrderData/Channel/text() ="CCP" or /ns0:OrderRequest/OrderData/Channel/text() ="RM" or /ns0:OrderRequest/OrderData/Channel/text() ="ASRM") and count(//Subscriber/CdbProfile)>0 and count(//Subscriber/CdbProfile/IMSI)>0 and //Subscriber/CdbProfile/IMSI/text()!=""

Waits for AA to confirm the DSD deactivation. Excluded for CCP, RM, and ASRM channels. Mirrors step 21's IMSI check.

27 MCS_CANCEL_AFTER_SALE — only when MCS_CANCEL_AFS=Y

FM: MCS_CANCEL_AFTER_SALE (not found)

boolean(//Subscriber[ExtendedInfo[Name='MCS_CANCEL_AFS' and Value='Y']])

Cancels the MCS after-sale package. Only triggered when subscriber carries the MCS_CANCEL_AFS='Y' extended info flag.

28 APIGW_OPT_IN_OUT — opt-out for CRM CBSC offers

FM: APIGW_OPT_IN_OUT (not found)

boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and Value='CRM'] and contains(SocProperties,'TR_OFFER_GROUP=CBSC')])

Opts out via API Gateway for subscribers with a CRM-managed CBSC offer group. ACTION=0 = opt-out.
