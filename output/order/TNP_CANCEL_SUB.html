Process Configuration for TNP_CANCEL_SUB — subscriber cancellation with KEEP_BAN_OPEN branching: L9 cancel (keeps BAN open) or standard cancel

Total Steps: 5 Unique FMs: 5 Entry: CCBS_SEARCH_SUBSCRIBER_BY_RESOURCE ✨ New FM docs: 1 Generated: 2026-09-23
Notable Pattern — KEEP_BAN_OPEN Branching
Mutually exclusive cancel paths (steps 4 & 5):
• KEEP_BAN_OPEN = 'Y' → Step 4: CCBS_L9_CANCEL_SUBS — L9 postpaid cancel (keeps BAN/billing account open)
• KEEP_BAN_OPEN = 'N' → Step 5: CCBS_CANCEL_SUBS — standard subscriber cancel (closes BAN)
Exactly one of the two executes per order.
