§1 — Process Overview Business & Technical Context

What does this process do?

POSTPAID_ADD_OFFER_SUB is the primary end-to-end order orchestration process for adding postpaid service offers to an existing subscriber. It is triggered when a customer selects a new add-on service (e.g. data pack, roaming, content subscription, or loyalty benefit) through any front-end channel (retail, BSNS enterprise portal, CCBS back-office, or third-party API).

The process follows a structured pipeline: first it collects and validates all customer, account, and subscriber data; then it resolves and prices the selected offers; then it provisions the services across multiple backend systems (CCBS, AA, SBM, MCS, Knox); and finally it notifies the customer and submits any required child orders to downstream OMX processes.
