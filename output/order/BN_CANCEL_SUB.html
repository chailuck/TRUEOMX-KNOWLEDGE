Notable Pattern — vs TNP_CANCEL_SUB
BN_CANCEL_SUB is the simpler variant of the cancel flows. Unlike TNP_CANCEL_SUB, there is no KEEP_BAN_OPEN branching — CCBS_CANCEL_SUBS executes unconditionally (no PreExecCheck on step 4). Used for BN (Broadband Network) subscriber cancellation.
