⚡ Mutual Exclusion Routing — BILL=Y / BILL=N
Steps 3 and 4 are mutually exclusive. After CCBS_GET_ACCOUNT_HEADER (step 2), the order routes to exactly one of:
  • Step 3 (AR credit) — when BILL=Y in SubscriberOffers ExtendedInfo
  • Step 4 (BL charge) — when BILL=N in SubscriberOffers ExtendedInfo
Both PreExecChecks are complementary — only one evaluates to true for any given order.

§3 — Conditional Execution Rules (PreExecCheck)
Step 1 — INTX_GET_PK_LIST_WITHOUT_RESOURCE_TYPE

FM: INTX_GET_PK_LIST_WITHOUT_RESOURCE_TYPE

boolean(//Subscriber[ExtendedInfo[Name='PRIMARY_RESOURCE_TYPE' and Value='C']])
and exists(//SubscriberOffers/ExtendedInfo[Name='BILL'])

Two-part gate: subscriber must be type C (cellular/mobile) AND at least one SubscriberOffer must have a BILL key. If either condition fails, the entire activity is skipped and the process moves to CCBS_GET_ACCOUNT_HEADER directly.

Step 3 — AR_L9_MULTI_CREATE_CHARGE_LEVEL_CREDIT (BILL=Y path) 
boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='BILL' and Value='Y']])

Routes to Amdocs AR charge-level credit when any SubscriberOffer has BILL=Y. Per offer with BILL=Y, sends an AR credit request (amount ← AMOUNT ExtendedInfo, taxInclude ← TAX_INCLUDE). Response writes CRM_COMPLETE_IND, CRM_CREDIT_ID, CRM_CREDIT_NOTE_NUMBER, CRM_ERROR_MESSAGE back to each offer.

Step 4 — BL_CREATE_CHARGE (BILL=N path) 
boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='BILL' and Value='N']])

Routes to BL charge creation when any SubscriberOffer has BILL=N. Mutually exclusive with step 3.
