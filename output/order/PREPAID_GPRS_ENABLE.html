§3 — Conditional Execution Rules (PreExecCheck)
Step 6 — CRM_CREATE_ORDER_PREPAID_ADD (FM: CRM_CREATE_ORDER_PREPAID)

FM: CRM_CREATE_ORDER_PREPAID

boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and (Value='BRMS' or Value='BRMS_REMOVE')]
  and ServiceType='85'])

Only creates the CRM order (AddRemoveOffer) if at least one SubscriberOffer has FE_OR_CCBS=BRMS or BRMS_REMOVE and ServiceType=85 (GPRS/Data service). This gates the CRM write so it only fires for relevant data-service offers from the BRMS rules engine.

Steps 8–10 — Provisioning gate: OMX_GET_SRV_TRX_NO, AA_PREPAID_PROVISIONING, AA_CHECK_CONFIRMATION

FMs: OMX_GET_SRV_TRX_NO, AA_PREPAID_PROVISIONING, AA_CHECK_CONFIRMATION

(not(boolean(/ns0:OrderRequest/OrderData[ExtendedInfo[Name='PROVISIONING']]))
  or boolean(/ns0:OrderRequest/OrderData[ExtendedInfo[Name='PROVISIONING' and Value!='N']]))
and (not(boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='EFF_TYPE']]))
  or boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='EFF_TYPE' and Value!='FUT']]))

The entire AA provisioning chain (get service transaction number → prepaid provisioning → confirmation) is gated by two conditions:

  • PROVISIONING flag: If the order carries an ExtendedInfo[PROVISIONING], its Value must not be 'N'. Allows upstream callers to suppress provisioning (e.g., dry-run or CCBS-only flows).
  • FUT offer gate: If any SubscriberOffer has EFF_TYPE, at least one must not be 'FUT' (future-dated). Skips provisioning for fully future-dated offer updates.
Step 11 — SMSGATEWAY_SEND_SMS

FM: SMSGATEWAY_SEND_SMS

boolean(//Subscriber[MSISDN/text()][1]
  and /ns0:OrderRequest/OrderData/Channel/text()!="CCBS"
  and /ns0:OrderRequest/OrderData/Channel/text()!="OMX")
and (not(boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='EFF_TYPE']]))
  or boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='EFF_TYPE' and Value!='FUT']]))

Sends SMS confirmation only if: MSISDN is present, the order did not originate from CCBS or OMX channels (suppresses internal/back-office flows), and the offer is not purely future-dated.
