§3 — Conditional Execution Rules (PreExecCheck Details)
Step 7 — OMX_INJECT_OFFER (PROCLRED removal gate) 
boolean(//Subscriber/SubscriberOffers[OfferName="PROCLRED" and ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and Value='CCBS']])

Fires only when subscriber has PROCLRED offer with FE_OR_CCBS=CCBS. Triggers CCD path (steps 15-19).

Steps 11-12 — Non-MSIM SUS AA provisioning 
not(MultiSIMInfo) and SubscriberType!="6A" and Status!="83"
Steps 13-14 — MSIM SUS AA provisioning 
MultiSIMInfo (Master or Minor PREV_MSIM) and SubscriberType!="6A" and Status!="83"
Steps 15-19 — CCD/PROCLRED path 
PROCLRED+INJECT_OFFER subscriber (subscriber has PROCLRED offer with INJECT_OFFER flag)
Step 20 — CCBS_SUSPEND_SUBS (non-CCBS channel) 
not(Channel="CCBS")

Skipped when Channel=CCBS. CCBS channel uses CCBS_APPLY_COLL_ACTIVITIES (step 22) instead.

Step 21 — OMX_ADD_FUT_CANCEL_SUBS (FCVG reason) 
ActivityReason="FCVG"

Schedules automatic cancellation N days after full suspension (convergence path only).

Step 22 — CCBS_APPLY_COLL_ACTIVITIES (CCBS channel only) 
Channel="CCBS"

Collection activities via CCBS. serviceType=FULL_SUSPEND → collectionAct=83, removes SOC 50412.

Steps 23-26 — Knox device locking sequence 
ActivityReason in (SUNOU, FSWC, AUTSU, PASUS, MANSU, COLL, AFRSU, CSUSR)
AND IMEI_KNOX ExtendedInfo is non-empty

4-step Knox locking flow: PSA_GET_DEVICE_INFO → KNOX_LOCK_DEVICE → OMX_NOTI_TO_KAFKA → PSA_UPDATE_KNOX_STATUS.

Step 27 — TDG_SUSPEND_SUBSCRIBER (IoT BU) 
TR_SPECIAL_OFFER_IND=IOTBU

IoT Business Unit subscribers only. Loops per MaterialInfo.Material (not per subscriber).

Steps 31-33 — ICC TVS Order variants

Three variants sharing the same FM (ICC_TVS_SUBMIT_ORDER):

  • Step 31 CVG: TVS_NO present → REASON=TFULL (uses TVS_NO from ATS step 30)
  • Step 32 BY_REQ: Channel!="CCBS" and trueIDTV ParameterInfo non-empty → REASON=TDISCOTT
  • Step 33 BY_COLLECTION: Channel="CCBS" and trueIDTV ParameterInfo non-empty → REASON=TFULLOTT
