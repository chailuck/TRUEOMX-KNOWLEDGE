ExtendedInfo[Name='SOURCE_OR_TARGET']/Value='SOURCE'

Step 8 — CCBS_GOD

Retrieve GOD offers only when FE or BRMS offers exist.

boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and (Value='FE' or Value='BRMS')]])
Step 9 — GET_SPECIAL_OFFER_INDICATOR (Param: ADD_PROP=TR_MULTISIM_IND)

Skips future-dated orders unless logical date provisioning is active. TR_MULTISIM_IND = RES/RCM/REE/RCE indicates MultiSIM resume scenarios.

(not(//ExtendedInfo[Name='EFF_TYPE']) or //ExtendedInfo[Name='EFF_TYPE']/Value!='FUT')
or boolean(//ExtendedInfo[Name='LOGICALDATE_PROV'])
Step 13 — ASRM_GET_NEXT_AVAILABLE_UNIFIED_RESOURCE_LIST (PROJ=RIO_SWAP, DEALERCODE=70000776)

TARGET subscriber who needs a new SIM but doesn't have FE SIM already assigned and isn't on the PMATCHID/eSIM path.

SOURCE_OR_TARGET='TARGET'
and not(ResourceInfo[ResourceName='SIM' and Source='FE'])
and not(ExtendedInfo[Name='NEW_PMATCHID'] or ExtendedInfo[Name='OLD_PMATCHID'])
Step 14 — ASRM_GET_RESERVE_UNIFIED_RESOURCE_LIST_ESIM (DEALERCODE=70000776)

eSIM subscriber needs ASRM reservation: has PMATCHID but not yet ICC_ID.

SOURCE_OR_TARGET='TARGET'
and string-length(ExtendedInfo[Name='NEW_PMATCHID']/Value)>0
and not(ExtendedInfo[Name='ICC_ID'])
Steps 15–16 — INTX_GET_SIM_INFO_BY_ICCID (PROJ=RIO_SWAP)

Step 15: eSIM PMATCHID path. Step 16: MultiSIM resume scenarios.

-- Step 15: SOURCE_OR_TARGET='TARGET' and ExtendedInfo[Name='NEW_PMATCHID']/Value>0
-- Step 16: ExtendedInfo[Name='TR_MULTISIM_IND']/Value in ('RES','RCM','REE','RCE')
Steps 17–19 — OMX_CAL_CHK_SUM / SMDP_PLUS_DOWNLOAD / SMDP_PLUS_CONFIRM (PROJ=RIO_SWAP)

SM-DP+ RIO/resume path — only when ICC_ID change checksum computed and SM-DP+ not yet confirmed.

-- Step 17: not(//ExtendedInfo[Name='SMDP_COMFIRMED' and Value='Y'])
-- Steps 18–19: string-length(//ExtendedInfo[Name='ICC_ID_CHG_SUM']/Value)>0
Steps 20, 22 — INTX_GET_CANCELLED_SIM / INTX_GET_MASTER_MINOR_SIM_INFO (MultiSIM resume)

Step 20 (RES/REE) — retrieve resume candidate cancelled SIMs. Step 22 (RES/RCM/REE/RCE, Param: CCD) — master/minor SIM info for MultiSIM linkage.

Step 23 — CCBS_MSIM_SWAP_SIM

Physical MultiSIM swap — TARGET subscriber with existing SOURCE SIM (OLD_SIM), not going through eSIM PMATCHID route.

SOURCE_OR_TARGET='TARGET'
and string-length(ExtendedInfo[Name='OLD_SIM']/Value)>0
and not(ExtendedInfo[Name='NEW_PMATCHID'] or ExtendedInfo[Name='OLD_PMATCHID'])
Steps 25–26 — SMDP_PLUS_DOWNLOAD_ESIM / SMDP_PLUS_CONFIRM_ESIM (PROJ=ESIM)

eSIM SM-DP+ download and confirm for pure eSIM (not RIO_SWAP) swap path.

SOURCE_OR_TARGET='TARGET'
and string-length(ExtendedInfo[Name='ICC_ID_CHG_SUM']/Value)>0
and not(ExtendedInfo[Name='SMDP_COMFIRMED' and Value='Y'])
Steps 27–28 — CCBS_ESIM_SWAP_SIM / CCBS_ESIM_SWAP_SIM_BULK ⚠ Response defect
⛔ Critical: Response rulefunction returns "true" unconditionally — no fan-in count check. May cause bulk swaps to appear complete prematurely.

Step 27 (non-bulk): TARGET with PMATCHID, no FILE_ID, no direct EID, not already called MSIM_SWAP_SIM.

Step 28 (bulk): FILE_ID present, TARGET, PMATCHID present.

Step 34 — CCBS_SWAP_SIM_POSTPAID_SUB ⚠ Typo in audit log

Postpaid SIM swap for TARGET subscribers who did NOT go through CCBS_MSIM_SWAP_SIM (IS_CALLED_MSIM_SWAP_SIM count=0). Handles IMSI + SIM update with GetSequenceValue for SOC_SEQ_NO. Known bug: OPERATION_NAME in request logger = "CCBS_SPWAP_SIM_POSTPAID_SUB" (typo).

Steps 36–40 — ASRM SIM lifecycle (RELEASE / AGE OUT / RESERVE / ACTIVATE / UPDATE_ATTRIBUTE)

Step 36 (RELEASE): SOURCE subscriber — release old SIM from ASRM.

Step 37 (AGE OUT on MINOR SIM): TARGET with OLD_EID (Source=FE) — age out old eSIM minor.

Step 38 (RESERVE): TARGET with no FE OLD_EID or not in REE/RCE, no PMATCHID.

Step 39 (ACTIVATE, PROJ=RIO): TARGET — activate SIM in ASRM.

Step 40 (UPDATE_ATTRIBUTE, EXPIRE_SELF=-1): TARGET with OLD_EID or REE/FE combination or PMATCHID.
