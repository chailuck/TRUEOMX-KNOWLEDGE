28 Steps 24 Unique FMs Entry: OMX_CREATE_SOURCE_STRUCTURE ★ 1 New FM Doc ⚠ 2 Internal Functions Generated: 2026-09-23
§1 — Process Overview

Prepaid SIM swap flow supporting both physical SIM swap and eSIM RIO swap paths. SOURCE subscriber data is read from CRM; TARGET subscriber is validated against ASRM & INTX. For eSIM, OMX computes a Luhn checksum (OMX_CAL_CHK_SUM ★) then triggers SM-DP+ download and confirm. Physical SIM swap bypasses the eSIM path via PreExecCheck. SIM lifecycle (RELEASE → RESERVE → ACTIVATE) is managed through ASRM. CRM order is created mid-flow after eSIM confirmation. Ends with SMS notification.
