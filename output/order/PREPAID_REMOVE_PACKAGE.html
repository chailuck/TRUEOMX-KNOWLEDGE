4 SBM_CANCEL_PACK_PREPAID — FM: SBM_CANCEL_PACK_PREPAID

Immediate cancellation (not deferred to next bill): FE offers with ServiceType=88/91 where EffectiveNextBillInd is absent or N.

boolean(//SubscriberOffers[
    ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and Value='FE']
    and (ServiceType='88' or ServiceType='91')
    and (not(exists(EffectiveNextBillInd)) or EffectiveNextBillInd = 'N')
])
5 SBM_CANCEL_PACK_PREPAID_NEXT — FM: SBM_CANCEL_PACK_PREPAID_NEXT

Deferred cancellation (next bill cycle): FE offers with ServiceType=88/91 where EffectiveNextBillInd=Y. Complement of step 4.

(boolean(//SubscriberOffers[
    ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and Value='FE']
    and (ServiceType='88' or ServiceType='91')
    and EffectiveNextBillInd = 'Y'
]))

§7 — ServiceType Routing Legend

ServiceType values determine which backend FM handles each SubscriberOffer in the removal flow:

ServiceTypeBackendFM(s)FE_OR_CCBSDescription
85OMXOMX_RESOLVE_SOC_DATAFEPrepaid SOC data — needs resolution before downstream removal
88SBMSBM_CANCEL_PACK_PREPAID / SBM_CANCEL_PACK_PREPAID_NEXTFESBM prepaid package — immediate (EffNextBillInd!=Y) vs. deferred (EffNextBillInd=Y)
91SBMSBM_CANCEL_PACK_PREPAID / SBM_CANCEL_PACK_PREPAID_NEXTFESBM prepaid package variant — same split as ServiceType=88
89CCPGET_PROFILE_FROM_CCP + CCP_REMOVE_OFFERFE (gate) / CCP (dynamically added)CCP price plan — step 6 queries grading tiers, step 7 removes all CCP offers
69MCSMCS_CANCEL_SUBSCRIPTIONFE or BRMSMCS managed subscription cancellation
GET_PROFILE_FROM_CCP pipeline note: GET_PROFILE_FROM_CCP (step 6) is a data enrichment step. It queries CCP's PricePlanDtoList and dynamically creates SubscriberOffers with FE_OR_CCBS=CCP and Action=Remove for each grading plan (IPP_000O0_00_GRADING prefix). These are then consumed by CCP_REMOVE_OFFER (step 7) along with the original FE-routed ServiceType=89 offers. Steps 6 and 7 share the same PreExecCheck because the gate condition for querying CCP is the same as for removing from CCP.
