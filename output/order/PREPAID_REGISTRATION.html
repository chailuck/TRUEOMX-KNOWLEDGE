§3 — Conditional Execution Rules (PreExecCheck)
Step 1 — CRM_GET_LAST_ASSET_ROOT
FM: CRM_GET_LAST_ASSET_ROOT
boolean(//Subscriber/MSISDN!="")
Executes only when the subscriber has a non-empty MSISDN. Skips if MSISDN is absent (no asset to look up).
Step 2 — CRM_GET_ASSET_COMPONENT_LIST
FM: CRM_GET_ASSET_COMPONENT_LIST
boolean(//Subscriber/AssetCrmId!="")
Executes only when an AssetCrmId was returned from step 1. Skips if CRM asset lookup yielded no ID.
Step 3 — OMX_GET_PARTIAL_ORDER_DATA
FM: OMX_GET_PARTIAL_ORDER_DATA
//Order/ExtendedInfo[Name='ORG_CHANNEL' and Value='3GW_iservice']
Executes only for orders originating from the 3GW iService channel (online self-service). Not executed for other channels.
Step 4 — CAT_GOD_A
FM: CAT_GOD (GET_SWITCH_FEATURE=N)
count(//Offers) > 0 or count(//SubscriberOffers) > 0
Executes when there is at least one Offer or SubscriberOffer — i.e., the order involves a product/tariff selection. Pure SIM-swap or admin orders with no offers skip this step.
Step 7 — CCP_ADD_HOME_ZONE
FM: CCP_ADD_HOME_ZONE
boolean(//OrderData[ExtendedInfo[Name="igoHomeZoneCode" and Value!=""]])
Executes only when the order carries a non-empty igoHomeZoneCode. Home zone is an optional tariff feature for fixed-mobile convergence.
Step 8 — OMX_RESOLVE_SOC_DATA
FM: OMX_RESOLVE_SOC_DATA
boolean(//OrderData[ExtendedInfo[Name="igoHomeZoneCode" and Value!=""]])
Same gate as step 7 — resolves SOC data for home zone offers. Only runs if home zone was also set in step 7.
Step 10 — CCP_GET_PREPAID_CREDIT_INFO
FM: CCP_GET_PREPAID_CREDIT_INFO
not(//SubscriberOffers/Soc/text()="10072810" and //SubscriberOffers/ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and ../Soc='10072810']/Value/text()='CCBS')
Skips when SOC 10072810 is present as a pure CCBS (old prepaid) offer. Only executes for non-CCBS or CCP-routed prepaid orders.
Step 11 — CAT_GOD_B
FM: CAT_GOD (GET_SWITCH_FEATURE=N)
(count(//Offers)>0 or count(//SubscriberOffers)>0) and boolean(//SubscriberOffers/ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and (Value='CCP' or Value='BRMS')])
Second CAT_GOD call — executes when offers exist AND the offer routing is CCP or BRMS. Retrieves switch feature configuration for CCP/BRMS-routed offers.
Step 12 — OMX_CAL_OFFER_FUT_DATE
FM: OMX_CAL_OFFER_FUT_DATE
boolean(//Subscriber/SubscriberOffers[1] and //SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and (Value='FE' or Value='BRMS')]])
Calculates future effective dates for FE or BRMS-routed offers. Skips for CCP-only orders.
Step 13 — OMX_ADD_FUT_OFFER
FM: OMX_ADD_FUT_OFFER (ORDERTYPE=69, USE_ROWID_CRM=Y)
boolean(//Subscriber/SubscriberOffers[1] and //SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and (Value='FE' or Value='BRMS')] and ExtendedInfo[Name='EFF_TYPE' and Value='FUT']])
Adds a future-dated offer (EFF_TYPE=FUT). Only when FE/BRMS routing AND the offer has a future effective type. ORDERTYPE=69 identifies prepaid future offer.
Step 14 — CAT_GET_SWF
FM: CAT_GET_SWF
(not(boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='EFF_TYPE']])) or boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='EFF_TYPE' and Value!='FUT']]))
Gets switch feature for immediately-effective offers. Skips when ALL offers are future-dated (EFF_TYPE=FUT).
Step 15 — OMX_GET_SRV_TRX_NO
FM: OMX_GET_SRV_TRX_NO (CCD)
//OrderData/Channel/text()!="TCC" and (not(boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='EFF_TYPE']])) or boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='EFF_TYPE' and Value!='FUT']]))
Gets service transaction number. Skips for TCC channel orders AND for future-only offer orders. CCD param indicates prepaid provisioning context.
Step 16 — AA_PREPAID_PROVISIONING
FM: AA_PREPAID_PROVISIONING (CCD)
not(//SubscriberOffers/Soc/text()="10072810" and FE_OR_CCBS[../Soc='10072810']/Value='CCBS') and //OrderData/Channel!="TCC" and (not(EFF_TYPE) or EFF_TYPE!='FUT')
Network provisioning for prepaid. Excludes CCBS-type SOC 10072810 orders, TCC channel, and future-only orders.
Step 17 — CCP_ACTIVATE_SIM
FM: CCP_ACTIVATE_SIM
//Order/ExtendedInfo[name='ACTIVATE_SIM' and value='Y']
SIM activation in CCP. Only triggered when the order explicitly carries ACTIVATE_SIM=Y extended info. Allows orders that do not need SIM activation to skip this step.
Step 18 — SBM_ADD_3GPREPAID
FM: SBM_ADD_3GPREPAID
boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and Value='FE'] and ServiceType='88'])
Adds 3G prepaid profile in SBM. Only for FE-routed offers with ServiceType=88 (prepaid mobile data).
Step 19 — CCP_ADD_OFFER
FM: CCP_ADD_OFFER
boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and (Value='FE' or Value='BRMS')] and ServiceType='89'])
Adds tariff plan offer in CCP. ServiceType=89 = prepaid tariff plan. Runs for FE or BRMS-routed offers.
Step 20 — CCP_CHANGE_PP
FM: CCP_CHANGE_PP
boolean(//SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and Value='FE'] and ServiceType='80'])
Changes price plan in CCP. ServiceType=80 = price plan. Only for FE-routed offers. BRMS-routed price plans go through a different path.
Step 22 — AA_CHECK_CONFIRMATION
FM: AA_CHECK_CONFIRMATION (CCD, UPDATE_NETWORK_STATUS)
not(//SubscriberOffers/Soc/text()="10072810" and FE_OR_CCBS[../Soc='10072810']/Value='CCBS') and //OrderData/Channel!="TCC"
Network confirmation check after provisioning. Skips for CCBS-type orders and TCC channel. UPDATE_NETWORK_STATUS param triggers status update post-confirmation.
Step 26 — MCS_REGISTER_SUBSCRIPTION
FM: MCS_REGISTER_SUBSCRIPTION (USE_ROWID_CRM=Y)
boolean(//Subscriber/SubscriberOffers[ExtendedInfo[Name='FE_OR_CCBS' and (Value='BRMS' or Value='FE')] and ServiceType='69'])
Registers subscription in MCS. ServiceType=69 = content/VAS subscription. Only for FE or BRMS-routed VAS offers. USE_ROWID_CRM=Y uses CRM row ID for linkage.
Step 27 — GPS_INSERT_INFORMATION
FM: GPS_INSERT_INFORMATION
string-length(//Customer/CustomerGeneralInfo/BirthDate)>0
Inserts birth date into GPS (geographic/personal subscriber data). Only runs when the customer's birth date is available. Required for prepaid registration identity records.
